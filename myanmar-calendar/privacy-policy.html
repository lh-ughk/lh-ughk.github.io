Privacy Policy

Myanmar Calendar

Last updated: 30 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Myanmar Calendar ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Myanmar Calendar is designed with privacy as a core principle — we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

1. Who we are

Myanmar Calendar is an offline calendar application developed by Cipher Studio. You can contact us at cipherstudio.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data we do NOT collect

Myanmar Calendar does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address).
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprints.
  • Location data.
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to a server.
  • Contacts or calendar entries from your device.

3. How the App works

All calendar data — Myanmar traditional dates, auspicious days, holidays, moon phases, and Thingyan festival dates — is bundled inside the App and computed entirely on your device. The App requires no internet connection and makes no network requests of any kind.

4. Permissions

Myanmar Calendar requests only the permissions necessary to operate. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, location, or device calendar. The App works fully without any special permissions beyond installation.

5. Third-party services

Myanmar Calendar does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services. There are no external SDKs that could collect data on our behalf.

6. Data storage

Any preferences you set within the App (such as language or display settings) are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage mechanisms. This data never leaves your device.

7. Children's privacy

Myanmar Calendar does not collect data from anyone, including children. The App is suitable for all ages. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws by design — because no data is collected at all.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date. Because no personal data is collected, no notification is required.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email cipherstudio.dev@gmail.com.
