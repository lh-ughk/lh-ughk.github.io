Features

Everything you need
to know your dates

Built for Myanmar — from the traditional calendar system to modern daily planning.

Myanmar Traditional Calendar

See every day in both the Gregorian and Myanmar Thingyan calendar systems. Full Myanmar date names, month names, and year count (မြန်မာသက္ကရာဇ်) displayed clearly for every date.

Auspicious Day Finder

Find the best dates for weddings, housewarmings, business openings, travel, and more. View why a day is favorable or unfavorable with traditional Myanmar astrology details.

Moon Phases

Waxing, full, waning, and new moon (လဆုတ်/လပြည့်/လဆန်း) tracked for every day in the Myanmar lunar calendar.

Buddhist Holy Days

Buddhist observance days (ဘုရားဖူး), full moon days, and Uposatha days clearly marked on every monthly view.

Myanmar Public Holidays

All Myanmar gazetted public holidays including Thingyan Water Festival, Independence Day, and more — never miss a day off.

Thingyan Festival

Exact Thingyan (သင်္ကြန်) start and end dates calculated automatically for every year — plan your festival in advance.

Bilingual Myanmar & English

Full support for Myanmar Unicode and English. Switch languages instantly. All day names, month names, and event labels shown in both scripts.

Fully Offline

No internet required, no account, no tracking. All calendar data is bundled in the app — open it anywhere, any time.
