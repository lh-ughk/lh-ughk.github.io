Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Strict no-activity-logs policy. We never monitor, record, or store what you do online.

1. Introduction

Secure Link VPN ("we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Secure Link VPN mobile application and all related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the Service and uninstall the app.

2. Our Core Commitment: No-Activity-Logs Policy

Secure Link VPN does not monitor, record, or store your online activity. When you are connected, we do not collect:

  • Websites you visit or content you access
  • Searches you perform
  • Apps you use while connected
  • Your originating IP address during a session
  • DNS queries
  • Data transfer content or packet information
  • Timestamps of individual browsing sessions

This is not a promise — it is a technical reality. Our servers are not configured to capture this data in the first place. There is nothing to hand over because there is nothing stored.

2.1 Connection Metadata

We retain only the minimum aggregated information needed to operate the Service:

  • Total data transferred during a billing period (to enforce plan quotas, not tied to specific sites visited)
  • Whether a VPN session was successfully established (success or failure — no browsing detail)
  • Server region you connected to (for capacity planning — not a log of your activity)

This aggregated data cannot be used to reconstruct your browsing history or identify specific sites you visited.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide you with a working, secure account.

Account Information

  • Email address — used for sign-in, account recovery, and service notifications
  • Display name — optional, used for your profile only
  • Profile photo — optional, sourced from Google Sign-In if you choose that method
  • Authentication method — whether you signed in with email/password or Google

Subscription and Billing Information

  • Your active plan type and subscription status
  • Subscription start date and renewal date
  • Purchase transaction reference IDs from Google Play
  • We do not see, store, or process your payment card details — all billing is handled entirely by Google Play

Device and App Information

  • App version and operating system version (for compatibility and troubleshooting)
  • Device model and manufacturer (to identify device-specific issues)
  • Crash reports and anonymous error logs (to fix bugs and improve reliability)
  • General app performance metrics (aggregate, not linked to your identity)

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your browsing history or search queries
  • The content of any data you transmit
  • Your real IP address during a VPN session
  • Location data (we do not request or access your GPS or location services)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive device resource

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide the Service — authenticate your account, manage your subscription, and give you access to VPN servers
  • Maintain and improve reliability — identify crashes, fix bugs, and improve connection stability
  • Manage your subscription — track your plan status, quota usage, and renewal dates
  • Send account notifications — important messages such as subscription expiry alerts or security notices (not marketing unless you opt in)
  • Enforce our Terms of Service — detect and prevent abuse that could affect other users
  • Comply with legal obligations — respond to lawful requests from government authorities where required

We do not sell, rent, share, or trade your personal information with any third party for commercial purposes. Ever.

5. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run the Service. Each is subject to its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase Authentication — manages your sign-in session and account credentials securely
  • Google Cloud Firestore — stores your account profile and subscription data, encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — receives anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs
  • Google Play Billing — processes all subscription payments; your payment details never touch our servers

We do not use advertising networks, social media trackers, or any analytics tools that share data with third parties.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Your account and subscription data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Authentication tokens on your device are stored using platform-standard secure storage provided by Android
  • All communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted connections
  • Access to our backend systems is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We conduct regular reviews of our security practices

No digital system is completely immune to attack. We take industry-standard precautions to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active
  • If you request account deletion, we remove your personal data within 30 days
  • Crash and error logs are automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Aggregated bandwidth usage data (quota tracking) is reset on each billing cycle
  • Session activity logs do not exist and therefore have no retention period

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to deletion — request that we delete your account and associated personal data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to object — object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Residents of the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority if they believe we have not handled their data appropriately.

9. Children's Privacy

Secure Link VPN is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Our services are operated globally and your data may be processed in countries other than the one in which you live. Where data is transferred outside your jurisdiction, we rely on appropriate safeguards — including Google's standard contractual clauses and data processing agreements — to protect it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We encourage you to review this Policy regularly. Your continued use of the Service after any changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
