QR Wave

Privacy Policy

Last updated June 4, 2026

Overview

QR Wave ("the app", "we", "us") is a QR code scanner and generator. We built QR Wave to be privacy‑first: your scan and generation history is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us. This policy explains what limited data is involved and how the third‑party services we rely on (advertising and in‑app purchases) handle information.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location. We do not operate servers that receive your scans, generated codes, or history. Camera frames used for scanning are processed on your device in real time and are never stored or transmitted by us.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your scan history, generated codes, favourites, and app settings are saved locally using on‑device storage. This data stays on your phone. You can delete individual items or clear all history at any time inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of QR Wave shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and ad interactions. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and partner policies. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings, and purchasing QR Wave Pro removes ads entirely.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

QR Wave Pro is an optional one‑time in‑app purchase processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; they are handled by Google. We store only a local flag indicating whether Pro is active so we can unlock features and hide ads.

Permissions We Request

Camera — required to scan QR codes and barcodes. Photos / Storage — used only when you choose to scan a code from an image or save a generated QR image to your gallery. Vibration — optional haptic feedback on a successful scan. Internet — required to load ads and open links you tap. Each permission is used solely for the feature described and only when you use that feature.

Links and Scanned Content

QR codes may contain links, Wi‑Fi credentials, contacts, or other data created by third parties. When you open a link or act on a scanned code, you leave QR Wave and interact with that third party. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those destinations — please scan and open codes only from sources you trust.

Children's Privacy

QR Wave is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because your content stays on your device, there is no central database of your data to breach. Network connections made by the app (for ads and opening links) use secure HTTPS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date and, where appropriate, highlighted in the app.
