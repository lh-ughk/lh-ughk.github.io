Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Thoughtfully designed to be fast and intuitive from the first tap.

Instant QR Generator

Create QR codes for URL, Text, Email, Phone, WiFi, vCard (Contact), Location, and Event — all 8 types generated in real time as you type.

Lightning-fast Scanner

Decode QR codes instantly with your camera, or pick any image from your gallery to scan — no camera needed. Results open directly in the right app.

Scan History & Favourites

Every scan is saved locally on your device. Revisit, copy, or re-share anytime. Star any entry to add it to your favourites for quick access.

Copy, Share & Save

Copy the QR content, share the QR image via any app, or save it directly to your photo gallery — one tap for each action.

Privacy First

Your data never leaves your device. No accounts, no servers. Camera frames processed locally, never stored.

Fast & Lightweight

Under 5 MB. Launches instantly, runs in the background without draining your battery.
