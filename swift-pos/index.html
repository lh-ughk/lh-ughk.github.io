Features

Everything a small
shop needs to sell

Built for speed at the counter and clarity at the close of day.

Fast Checkout

Ring up sales in seconds with quick product search, multi-item carts, discounts, taxes and delivery details — then print or share a receipt.

Inventory & Stock

Manage products, categories and prices, track stock levels and inventory movements, and get a clear view of what's running low.

Customers & Staff

Keep a customer directory and create staff accounts with roles. Each user signs in with a secure, hashed PIN.

Expenses & Shifts

Record expenses, open and close shifts, and reconcile the cash drawer at the end of every day.

Receipts & Reports

Generate receipts for every sale and review daily summaries — sales, top sellers, transactions and totals at a glance.

Offline-first

Everything works without internet. Your data lives on the device, so the counter never stops — even when the connection does.

Secure Cloud Backup

Sign in with a cloud account to back up and sync your shop across devices, protected by row-level security on Supabase.

Privacy & Security

No advertising IDs, no location tracking, no data selling. PINs are salted and hashed, and secrets stay in the device's secure storage.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

No complex setup. Create your shop and start selling.

01

Set up your shop

Add your shop profile, products and prices. Create staff accounts with their own secure PINs.

02
