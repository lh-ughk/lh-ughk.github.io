Data deletion request

You can permanently delete your Swift POS account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Swift POS app and sign in as the owner.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your owner account (email/login).
  • Your shop profile and settings.
  • All products, categories, inventory and stock history.
  • All sales, receipts, customers, expenses and shift records.
  • All staff users and their PIN credentials.
  • All local data on the device.

Nothing is retained after deletion. This action cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email cipherstudio.dev@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my Swift POS account". We will verify ownership and delete your data within 30 days.
