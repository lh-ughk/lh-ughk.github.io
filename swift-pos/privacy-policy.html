Privacy Policy

Swift POS — offline-first Point of Sale

Last updated: 8 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swift POS ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

Swift POS is a point-of-sale application for small retail businesses. The developer can be contacted at cipherstudio.dev@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a. Account information

  • Owner email address and a securely hashed password (used for cloud sign-in and backup). Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in plain text.
  • Shop name, shop type, address, phone number and other shop profile details you enter.
  • Staff names, roles and a salted, hashed PIN for each user. PINs are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

b. Business data you create

  • Products, categories, prices, stock and inventory movements.
  • Sales, receipts, discounts, taxes and delivery details.
  • Customers, expenses and shift/cash-drawer records.
  • Optional product/receipt images you add.

c. Technical data

  • Basic device and diagnostic information needed to operate and synchronise the App (e.g. connectivity status and sync timestamps).

We do not collect location data, advertising identifiers, contacts, or sell your data to anyone.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide core POS features (selling, inventory, reports).
  • To authenticate users and protect access with PIN/biometric locks.
  • To back up and synchronise your shop across your devices when you are online.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

4. Offline-first storage

The App is offline-first: your data is stored locally on your device and works without internet. When you sign in with a cloud account and are online, data is synchronised to our cloud provider so you can restore it or use multiple devices.

5. Where your data is stored

Cloud data is hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL), which acts as our data processor. Access is protected by row-level security so that each shop can only access its own data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by the hosting provider. See Supabase's privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

6. Data sharing

We share data only with infrastructure providers strictly necessary to run the App (our cloud database/authentication provider). We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), your shop data is permanently removed from our cloud and from the device.

8. Deleting your account & data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the App:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

This erases your shop, products, sales, customers, staff and all other data from this device and from the cloud. The action is irreversible. You can also request deletion by email at cipherstudio.dev@gmail.com — see our account deletion page.

9. Security

  • PINs are protected with a salted, iterated hash and never leave the device in plain text.
  • Cloud access is governed by row-level security scoped to your shop.
  • Local secrets are kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore).

10. Children's privacy

Swift POS is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Your rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of this is available directly in the App; for anything else, contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

Questions? Email cipherstudio.dev@gmail.com.
